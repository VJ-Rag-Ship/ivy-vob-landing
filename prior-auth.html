The problem

Prior authorization is the biggest bottleneck between prescribing a biologic and the patient getting it.

Every Xolair or Dupixent start needs a stack of forms, proof of step therapy, a payer portal, and a 3 to 14 day wait. In most practices, a full-time staff member is doing this all day.

01 · SLOW

40 minutes per case

Chart review, form filling, portal navigation, and follow-up calls together take 30 to 60 minutes for every biologic prior authorization.

02 · DENIED

1 in 4 gets denied first pass

Missing documents, ICD-10 mismatches, step-therapy proof errors. Between 15 and 25 percent of biologic prior auths get denied the first time. Every denial means another appeal.

03 · DELAYED

Patients wait weeks

Payers take 3 to 14 days to decide. If they deny, the cycle starts over. Patients often wait weeks for their first injection. Some give up before they get it.

04 · MANUAL

Two to four hours per appeal

For every denied case, someone has to pull the chart, cite the payer’s policy, write the letter, and fax it. That work runs two to four hours per patient.

How it works
