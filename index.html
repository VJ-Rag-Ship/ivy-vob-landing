Common questions

Everything you want to know.

How do I get started?
Book a 20-minute call with our team. We'll walk through your EHR setup, payer mix, and a 1-week rollout plan. Most practices run their first live verification in under 5 days from kickoff.
How long does integration take?
Day one for File Upload and Instant Verification modes. 3–7 days for Auto-Sync to your EHR. Ivy is fully EHR agnostic, no technical integration project required from your side.
Does Ivy cover all my payers?
Ivy is live across 125+ plans on UnitedHealthcare, Cigna, Aetna, Anthem BCBS, Medicare, and government payers. If a payer in your mix isn't covered yet, we add it for you, typically within 2 weeks. Tricare, Humana, Kaiser, and Centene are next in queue.
How much does Ivy cost?
Pricing is volume-based: a transparent per-verification rate that scales down as your practice grows. Most practices see ROI in the first month. Use the savings calculator above to see your numbers, then book a call for exact pricing.
Is Ivy HIPAA-compliant?
Yes. RagaAI is HIPAA, SOC 2, and ISO 27001 certified. All patient data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). BAA available on request. We never train models on your data.
What happens when Ivy isn't confident about a verification?
Every verification is AI confidence-scored. High-confidence cases auto-flow to a generated document and patient email. Low-confidence cases are flagged for your staff with specific data gaps highlighted and a one-click manual trigger. Your team handles the exceptions, not the routine work.
Who built Ivy?
Ivy was built in close partnership with leading allergists, including Dr. Gary N. Gross (Dallas Allergy & Asthma) and other practicing physicians on our healthcare council. The team sat in clinics, watched the phone calls, counted the denials, and built what should have existed years ago.
