The problem

Traditional collections is broken.

Statement cycles are slow, paper is expensive, agencies keep a third of what they recover, and the whole thing runs on staff hours you don’t have.

01 · SLOW

30 to 45 day cycle

Paper and e-statements run a 30–45 day payment cycle before anyone pays. Balances age. Some age out.

02 · EXPENSIVE

$0.50–$1.00 per statement

Each mailed statement costs money to print, stuff, and send. No guarantee it gets paid. Repeat for every cycle.

03 · COSTLY LATE

25–50% agency cut

Once a balance escalates, collection agencies typically keep 25 to 50% of whatever they recover.

04 · MANUAL

Hours every week

Staff spend hours a week chasing balances by hand. It doesn’t scale as the practice grows.

Patient journey

Four steps, every card charged with consent.

Every patient gets a chance to self-pay first. Card-on-file check only runs on non-payers.

01

Build the list

Pull outstanding balances directly from the EHR. Active payment-plan patients are automatically excluded. Nothing manual.

02

Smart reminders

Text messages go out with a secure payment link. Timing adapts automatically to what’s converting for that patient and that practice.

03

Card check

For anyone who still hasn’t paid, check whether a card is on file. Non-payers only. Never touched before the reminder sequence runs.

04

Resolve

Consent-backed charge on the card on file, or for the smallest remaining slice, escalate to collections. Every step logged.

Guarantee
No card is ever checked before the reminder sequence runs. Every patient gets a fair chance to self-pay first. Card charges only happen after reminders have gone unanswered, and never without documented consent and a 24-hour notice.

Why allergy & asthma

Small balances, high frequency. Perfect for automation.

Immunotherapy makes this a volume game.

Frequent visits (allergy shots, biologics infusions, follow-ups) mean many small recurring patient balances. Expensive to chase manually. Cheap to automate.

The kicker: small balances convert best. Balances under $30 pay at 7.2% when nudged by SMS. Balances over $700 pay at just 0.9%. Volume is the friend, not the enemy.
